Who we serve
We serve adults who already want to exercise but repeatedly fail to stay consistent week after week. The strongest fit is irregular exercisers, restarters, and overcommitters who need realistic planning, recovery after misses, and help turning intention into follow-through.

Who we do not serve
We do not serve people mainly looking for workout content, elite training programming, or a broad wellness OS on day one. We also do not optimize for users who want a passive tracker, a streak counter, or a chatty AI that gives generic motivation without changing the plan.

Primary problem
The core problem is not finding workouts; it is sustaining exercise behavior in real life when energy, time, confidence, and routines break down. Existing tools often help users start, but they do not reliably help users recover, adapt, and continue.

Core promise
We help people complete planned workouts more consistently by adapting the plan to their actual behavior and capacity. The promise is simple: better follow-through, fewer guilt spirals, and a routine that survives missed days instead of collapsing.

Why users choose us
Users choose us because we make exercise more doable, not just more visible. We help them create believable plans, recover after lapses, and get a next step that fits the week they are actually living.

One-sentence positioning statement
Exercise Consistency Coach helps irregular exercisers stay consistent by adapting workout plans to real behavior, capacity, and recovery needs.

Elevator pitch
Most fitness apps help you log workouts or browse plans; we help you actually keep exercising. We watch what happens, detect when the plan is too hard, too rigid, or badly timed, and then adjust it so the user can keep going instead of dropping off.

Future vision
The long-term vision is a Human Behavior Platform that applies the same Goal-Plan-Activity-Daily State-Adaptation model to exercise first, then to other repeat behaviors only after the exercise wedge is proven. Expansion is a privilege earned by retention and adherence wins, not a launch narrative.

What we refuse to become
We refuse to become a broad wellness app, a workout-content marketplace, a streak-punishing tracker, or an AI companion that sounds helpful but does not change outcomes. We also refuse to optimize for daily engagement if it comes at the cost of long-term adherence, trust, or strategic focus.

Strategic guardrails
The company should be judged by whether it improves workout follow-through, not by feature breadth or chat usage. If a feature does not improve activation, adherence, lapse recovery, or paid conversion, it belongs outside the core product.

Founder rule
If a proposed idea would make us look more like a fitness app, a habit app, or a generic AI coach, it is probably drift. If it makes the user more likely to complete the next planned workout and return after a miss, it is probably core.
